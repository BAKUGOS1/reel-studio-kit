> Attribution: `hook-archetypes.md` is from advertising-skills by Nativa Labs, https://github.com/mbfinotti/advertising-skills. Licence: MIT. Details in `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

The first 2 seconds of a reel: the triad (voice, on-screen text and first frame each carry different information), the 13 hook archetypes in three tiers, the per-hook quality bar (competitor test, Four U's, under 2 s spoken, 3-7 words on screen, works muted) and opening-frame anti-patterns.

`hook-archetypes.md` was written for UGC creators talking to camera, so read it this way for narrated reels:

- **The triad** applies to narrated reels: the **voice line**, the **on-screen text** and the **first frame** each carry different
  information. The on-screen text never captions the voice.
- **"Talent to camera"** applies only if your reels show a real presenter. Otherwise the rest holds: open on motion, a real screen, or the result.
- **Tier 1** (skeptic's turn, problem callout, POV) suits story reels and POV reels. **Contrarian** suits concept/argument reels
  ("Until now."). **Proof-first / silent review** suits SaaS demos (the real product screen is the receipt).
- **Competitor test:** if a competitor or an adjacent service could say the opening word for word, rewrite it.
- **B2B skew** section: use it only if your audience is businesses.
- Where the file mentions "the skill's interview", it means the original ugc-ad-scripts skill; ask the owner those questions directly instead.
