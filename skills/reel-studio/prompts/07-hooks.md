# 07 · Hooks and the first two seconds

For: Claude. Run on the logline or the draft script; pick with the owner; test one variable at a time. The full method is the story engine's step 6 (`playbooks/story-engine.md`) and `guides/hooks/hook-archetypes.md`.

```text
For this story reel: [LOGLINE], give 10 hook options as a table:
# | spoken line (in the reel's language, under 8 words) | on-screen question (under 6 words) |
first frame (the face, its expression, what the stopwatch shows) | type (question / stake / confession / number /
contradiction) | why a viewer in [AUDIENCE] would stop scrolling.
Mix the types. No clickbait the reel doesn't pay off; every hook must be answered by the release.
The spoken line, the on-screen text and the first frame each carry different information.
Then design the first 2 seconds of the best one: the spoken line with its [audio tag], the on-screen text and
where it sits (inside 168–1330 px), the first visual frame, the first movement, the first sound, and the pattern
interrupt at about 1.5 s.
```
