# 02 · Character sheet (one per new person)

For: the owner in Codex, before any shot with that person. Attach the existing series sheets (`src/<slug>/assets/art/00-*-sheet.png` from earlier reels) so the new person sits in the same world. Save as `src/<slug>/assets/art/00-<name>-sheet.png` (1536×1024).

```text
[STYLE LOCK — paste prompts/01, with even, flat light for a sheet]

Character turnaround sheet on plain cream paper: [NAME, ROLE], about [AGE].
Look: [height and build], [face shape], [hair, how it's worn today], [facial hair or none], [glasses or none],
[skin tone], [clothes for this scene, with one personal detail: a watch, a shawl, a ring], [one habit or prop].
Personality in the pose: [e.g. tired but proud; quick to joke].
Show: front view, three-quarter view, and three head studies with the mouth closed, mouth open (talking), and eyes
closed. Same lighting on every view. Full body on the turnarounds.
This person must be instantly distinguishable from [OTHER CHARACTERS IN THE SERIES] at a glance:
different [height / build / hairline / facial hair / glasses].
No text or name labels on the sheet.

Reject if: the face differs between views, any lettering appears, extra or merged fingers, or the person looks
like an existing series character.
```
