# 09 · Caption, search line, hashtags and pinned comment

For: Claude, when the reel is final. Paste the brand context block first. Output goes into `reels/<slug>/caption.md` with the alt text.

```text
Write the Instagram post package for this reel: [LOGLINE + WHAT THE REAL SCREEN SHOWS].
1. FIRST LINE (shows before "more"): the story in under 12 words, in the audience's language, a hook not a summary.
2. BODY (3–5 short lines): the human moment; one line on what the product does, quoted from [CLAIM SOURCE];
   the CTA from the brand context (link in bio).
3. SEND PROMPT: "Send this to [the person it's about]."
4. NEXT STORY line and a comment question about the viewer's own life.
5. SEARCH LINE: one natural sentence with the words people search for this topic [e.g. wedding budget,
   wedding planning, guest list], no stuffing.
6. HASHTAGS: 5–8, mixing broad and niche, plus the brand's own tag.
7. PINNED COMMENT: a question that invites a story ("Who in your family …?").
8. ALT TEXT: what happens on screen, start to end, including that the app screen shows demonstration data.
9. CREDITS: the creator of any reference reel this one learned from.
No invented numbers, no fake scarcity, no engagement bait ("comment YES").
```
